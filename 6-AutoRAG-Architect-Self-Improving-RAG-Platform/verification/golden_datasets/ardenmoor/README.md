# Benchmark: ardenmoor

## Purpose

Golden-dataset benchmark grounded in a fictional annual report, for manual pipeline
ingestion testing and the MVP demo. The same PDF this dataset is built from
(`source/Ardenmoor_Annual_Report_FY2025.pdf`) is also the document the user plans to
manually ingest into the running AutoRAG pipeline — so this benchmark doubles as the
ground-truth answer key for that same corpus.

## Source Document

`source/Ardenmoor_Annual_Report_FY2025.pdf` — a **wholly fictional 113-page annual
report** for a non-existent company ("Ardenmoor Industries Corporation", NYSE: ARDX),
created for software testing and demonstration purposes only. Every page footer states
this explicitly ("Fictional company — for software testing purposes only"). No real
company, person, or financial data is represented.

The report covers: company overview & history, business model, global operations,
manufacturing facilities (21 plants), products & services, market overview, competitive
landscape, sustainability (ESG), corporate governance, risk management, MD&A, business
segment performance, financial highlights, income statement, balance sheet, cash flow
statement, statement of changes in equity, financial notes, shareholding pattern,
dividend policy, subsidiaries, future outlook, glossary, and appendices A–P (segment
detail, plant directory, workforce by country, product catalog, illustrative auditor's
report, investor info, corporate timeline, certifications, ESG by plant, five-year
segment trends, regional financial detail, customer case studies, non-GAAP
reconciliations, environmental data, employee message, FAQ, list of tables/figures).

## Question Distribution

30 questions total. See `metadata.json` for the machine-readable distribution and
`reports/coverage_report.md` (generated by `evaluation/generators/export_dataset.py`) for
the full breakdown with pass/fail validation.

- Categories (8): Company Overview, Business Segments & Products, Financial Statements,
  Financial Ratios & KPIs, Governance & Leadership, Risk Management,
  Sustainability (ESG), Corporate Actions — 3-4 questions each.
- Difficulty: 13 Easy / 13 Medium / 4 Hard.

## Citation Rules

- `page_number` is the **printed footer page number** visible on the page (e.g. "24"),
  not the raw PDF page index. The PDF page index runs 2 higher than the footer number
  throughout — cover + table of contents occupy the first 2 unnumbered pages — so
  `PDF page = footer page + 2`. Retrieval evaluation code that jumps to a citation by raw
  PDF page index must apply this offset.
- `section` cites the report's own numbered section headings (e.g. "13 Risk Management",
  "27 Appendix A: Segment Financial Detail").

## Known Limitations

- All 30 questions carry `review_status: generated` — not yet human-reviewed.
- Several questions (GT-020, GT-022, GT-026, GT-030) intentionally span content that
  crosses a page boundary or two separate passages (multi-hop / cross-table), to give the
  retrieval evaluation genuine difficulty beyond single-chunk lookup.
- GT-018 documents a minor internal inconsistency in the source PDF itself: the
  Corporate Governance Framework diagram shows Audit, Risk, and Nomination & Governance
  as three separate committee boxes, while the accompanying prose states "three standing
  committees: Audit & Risk, Compensation, and Nomination & Governance" (combining Audit
  and Risk into one). The ground truth follows the explicit prose statement.

## Generation Process

Hand-authored by an AI engineering agent (24-Jul-2026) after reading all 113 pages of the
source PDF in 20-page batches. Validated against `schema/golden_dataset.schema.json` via
`jsonschema.Draft202012Validator` (0 errors, no duplicate IDs).
