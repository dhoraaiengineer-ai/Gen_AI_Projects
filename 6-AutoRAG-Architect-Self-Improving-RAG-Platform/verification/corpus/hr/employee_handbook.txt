EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK — AUTORAG SOLUTIONS PTE LTD
Version 3.2 | Effective Date: 01 January 2026

SECTION 1 — INTRODUCTION

Welcome to AutoRAG Solutions. This handbook outlines the policies, procedures, and expectations that govern employment at our organization. All employees are expected to read, understand, and comply with the contents of this handbook. Questions should be directed to the People Operations team at people@autorag-solutions.com.

The company operates across three primary offices: Bengaluru (headquarters), Singapore, and remote. All employees regardless of location are subject to the same code of conduct and core policies described herein.

SECTION 2 — EMPLOYMENT CLASSIFICATIONS

Full-time employees work a minimum of 40 hours per week and are eligible for all company benefits including health insurance, provident fund contributions, and paid leave.

Part-time employees work fewer than 30 hours per week and are eligible for pro-rated leave entitlements. They are not eligible for company-sponsored health insurance unless they exceed 25 hours per week on a sustained basis.

Contract employees are engaged for a defined term or specific project. They are not eligible for company benefits unless explicitly stated in their individual contracts. Contract employees must adhere to the same code of conduct as permanent staff.

Probationary employees are subject to a 90-day probation period during which either party may terminate employment with 7 days written notice. Upon successful completion of probation, the standard notice periods apply.

SECTION 3 — WORK HOURS AND ATTENDANCE

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM local time, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour unpaid lunch break. Flexible working arrangements may be approved by the direct manager and People Operations.

Employees are expected to notify their manager at least 30 minutes before their scheduled start time if they are unable to attend work. Repeated unplanned absences may result in disciplinary action.

Remote work is permitted for up to 3 days per week for eligible roles. Eligibility is determined by role type and team requirements. All remote employees must be available during core hours of 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in their designated time zone.

Overtime must be pre-approved by the line manager. Non-exempt employees will be compensated at 1.5 times their standard hourly rate for hours worked beyond 40 per week.

SECTION 4 — COMPENSATION AND PERFORMANCE REVIEWS

Salary reviews are conducted annually in March. Increases are based on individual performance ratings, market benchmarking, and company financial performance. The performance rating scale uses five levels: Exceptional, Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Partially Meets Expectations, and Does Not Meet Expectations.

Employees rated Exceptional or Exceeds Expectations are eligible for merit increases of up to 15% and annual bonuses of up to 20% of base salary. Employees rated Meets Expectations receive merit increases of up to 8% with no bonus guarantee.

Employees rated Partially Meets Expectations or Does Not Meet Expectations will be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan within 30 days of the review cycle. They are not eligible for merit increases during the plan period.

SECTION 5 — LEAVE ENTITLEMENTS (SUMMARY)

Annual leave: 18 days per year for employees with less than 3 years tenure; 21 days for 3 to 7 years; 25 days for more than 7 years.
Sick leave: 12 days per year, non-accumulative.
Maternity leave: 26 weeks fully paid for the primary caregiver.
Paternity leave: 4 weeks fully paid.
Bereavement leave: 5 days for immediate family; 2 days for extended family.
Public holidays: As per the official calendar for each office location.

Full details on leave policies are provided in the Leave Policy document.

SECTION 6 — GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE

Employees who have a workplace concern should first attempt to resolve it informally with their immediate manager. If this is unsuccessful or the concern involves the manager, the employee should escalate to People Operations within 10 business days.

A formal investigation will be initiated within 5 business days of a written complaint. All parties will be treated with fairness and confidentiality. Retaliation against employees who raise legitimate grievances is strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

SECTION 7 — DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURE

Minor infractions result in a verbal warning documented in the employee file. Repeated minor infractions or a single serious infraction may result in a written warning. Gross misconduct may result in immediate termination without prior warning, subject to applicable local law.

Examples of gross misconduct include: theft, fraud, workplace violence, harassment, discrimination, and serious breach of data security policies.
