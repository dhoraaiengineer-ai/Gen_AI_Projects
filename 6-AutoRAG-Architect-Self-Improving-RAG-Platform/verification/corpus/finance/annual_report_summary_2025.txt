RISK FACTORS

LLM Dependency: The company relies on third-party LLM providers (Gemini, KiMi). Significant price increases, service disruptions, or changes in provider terms could adversely affect operating costs and service reliability. The multi-provider failover architecture mitigates availability risk but not price risk.

Regulatory Risk: Increasing AI regulation across the EU (AI Act), Singapore (Model AI Governance Framework), and India (DPDP) may require additional compliance investments. The company has allocated SGD 0.4 million in FY2026 for regulatory compliance capabilities.

Competition: The enterprise RAG platform market is growing rapidly with well-funded competitors. The company's key differentiators are the autonomous Unified Score optimization loop and the LangGraph-based deterministic orchestration, which competitors have not replicated.
