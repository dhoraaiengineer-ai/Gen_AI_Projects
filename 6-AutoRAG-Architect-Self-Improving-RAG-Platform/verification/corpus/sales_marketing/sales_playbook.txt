1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW FOR SALES

AutoRAG Architect is an enterprise SaaS platform that lets organizations build AI systems that can answer questions from their own documents — accurately, with source citations, and with automatic quality verification.

The key differentiator is the Unified Score system: an autonomous optimization loop that continuously improves retrieval quality without manual tuning. Competitors require weeks of engineering effort to optimize RAG pipelines; AutoRAG does it automatically.

Primary use cases:
  - Internal knowledge management: employees asking questions of HR policies, IT runbooks, and internal wikis
  - Customer support: AI-powered support agents trained on product documentation and FAQs
  - Regulatory compliance: legal and compliance teams querying policies and regulatory documents
  - Technical documentation: engineering teams querying API specs, runbooks, and architecture guides
  - Financial analysis: finance teams querying reports, contracts, and financial statements

2. IDEAL CUSTOMER PROFILE (ICP)

Primary ICP:
  - Company size: 500 to 10,000 employees
  - Industry: Technology, Financial Services, Healthcare, Professional Services
  - Signals: Currently using basic ChatGPT plugins or early RAG prototypes; frustrated with hallucinations or poor retrieval accuracy; have a dedicated AI/ML or data engineering team
  - Budget: Annual contract value USD 50,000 to USD 500,000
  - Geography: Singapore, India, Southeast Asia (primary); UAE, UK (secondary expansion markets)

Secondary ICP:
  - Mid-market companies (100 to 499 employees) with a strong AI-first culture
  - Global systems integrators building RAG capabilities for their clients

Disqualify if:
  - They need a general-purpose chatbot without document grounding (recommend a competitor or ChatGPT)
  - They have fewer than 100 employees with no dedicated AI team
  - They cannot provide documents in digital format (scanned paper archives without OCR)

4. COMPETITIVE POSITIONING

vs. Building in-house:
  - Building a production-quality RAG pipeline from scratch takes 3 to 6 months of engineering time
  - AutoRAG delivers a production-ready pipeline in hours to days
  - AutoRAG includes built-in evaluation, monitoring, and improvement that most in-house builds lack
  - Total cost of ownership is lower: no engineering maintenance burden

vs. Generic vector database tools (Pinecone, Weaviate):
  - Vector databases are components, not complete solutions
  - AutoRAG provides the full stack: ingestion, chunking, embedding, retrieval, generation, evaluation, and deployment
  - AutoRAG's Unified Score evaluation is not available in raw vector database offerings

vs. Other RAG platforms:
  - Most competitors require manual hyperparameter tuning; AutoRAG automates this
  - AutoRAG's adversarial testing catches hallucinations before they reach production
  - AutoRAG's LangGraph orchestration provides deterministic, auditable pipeline behavior
