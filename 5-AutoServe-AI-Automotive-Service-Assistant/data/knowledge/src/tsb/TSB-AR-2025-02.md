## TSB-AR-2025-02 Air conditioning not cold enough at idle
Affected vehicles: Veloxa Aria, model years 2022–2024, VIN serials 100001–100040 (SYNTHETIC).
Symptom: Air conditioning blows warm air when stationary or in slow traffic; normal at speed. B1A23 may be stored.
Cause: The condenser fan relay contacts may stick open when hot.
Correction: Replace the condenser fan relay with the updated part VLX-AC-RLY-03 (op code OP-AC-RELAY). Confirm the fan runs with the A/C on at idle.
