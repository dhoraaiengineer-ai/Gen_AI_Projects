## TSB-TR-2025-03 Steering wheel vibration at highway speed
Affected vehicles: Veloxa Terra, model years 2023–2025, VIN serials 100001–100050 (SYNTHETIC).
Symptom: Steering wheel vibration between 100 and 120 km/h. No DTCs stored.
Cause: Corrosion on the hub face prevents the wheel seating flat, causing run-out.
Correction: Clean the hub faces and wheel mating surfaces, then balance all four wheels (service manual section 7.5, op code OP-WHL-BAL). Inspect tyres for uneven wear; tyre wear is not covered by warranty.
