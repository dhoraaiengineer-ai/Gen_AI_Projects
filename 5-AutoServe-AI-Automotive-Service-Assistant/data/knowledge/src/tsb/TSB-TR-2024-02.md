## TSB-TR-2024-02 DPF regeneration incomplete on short trips
Affected vehicles: Veloxa Terra, model years 2022–2024, VIN serials 100001–100050 (SYNTHETIC).
Symptom: DPF light comes on repeatedly; P2463 stored; customer drives mainly short urban trips.
Cause: Engine control software ends passive regeneration too early at low exhaust temperatures.
Correction: Update the engine control software to version TR-ECU-4.2 (op code OP-ECU-FLASH), then perform a forced regeneration (service manual section 7.1) if the soot load is below the replacement limit.
