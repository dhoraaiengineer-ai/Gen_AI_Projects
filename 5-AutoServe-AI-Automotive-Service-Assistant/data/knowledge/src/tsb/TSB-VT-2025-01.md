## TSB-VT-2025-01 AC charging stops before the set charge limit
Affected vehicles: Veloxa Volt, model years 2023–2025, VIN serials 100001–100060 (SYNTHETIC).
Symptom: AC home charging stops at about 80% even when the charge limit is set to 100%. P0D27 may be stored. DC fast charging is not affected.
Cause: Onboard charger software misreads the charge limit after a wall box restart.
Correction: Update the onboard charger software to version VT-OBC-2.7 (op code OP-OBC-UPDATE). First confirm the customer's charge limit setting (owner's manual section 4.1), because stopping at the set limit is normal.
