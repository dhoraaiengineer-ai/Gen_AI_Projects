## TSB-VT-2025-02 Infotainment screen freezes
Affected vehicles: Veloxa Volt, model years 2023–2024, VIN serials 100001–100060 (SYNTHETIC).
Symptom: The centre touchscreen freezes for up to two minutes; climate controls may not respond. U0164 may be stored.
Cause: A memory leak in infotainment software version VT-INFO-1.9.
Correction: Update the infotainment and HVAC software to VT-INFO-2.1 (op code OP-INFO-UPDATE).
