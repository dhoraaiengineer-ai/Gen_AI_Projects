## TSB-AR-2024-01 Rough idle on cold start
Affected vehicles: Veloxa Aria, model years 2021–2023, VIN serials 100001–100030 (SYNTHETIC).
Symptom: Rough idle or shaking for up to one minute after a cold start. The engine warning light may come on with P0300 or P0301 stored.
Cause: The cylinder 1 ignition coil connector locking clip may not fully engage, causing an intermittent connection when cold.
Correction: Replace the ignition coil connector clip and apply dielectric grease. Op code OP-TSB-AR01. Do not replace the ignition coil unless the misfire continues after this repair.
