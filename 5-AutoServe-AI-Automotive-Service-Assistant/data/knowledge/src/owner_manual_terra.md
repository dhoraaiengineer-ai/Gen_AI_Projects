## 1.1 About this manual
This is a SYNTHETIC excerpt for the fictional Veloxa Terra diesel SUV. It is for demonstration only and must not be used for any real vehicle.

## 3.1 Engine warning light (amber)
Meaning: The engine management system has detected a fault that may affect emissions or performance.
Required action: Reduce speed and avoid heavy loads or towing. Contact Veloxa service within the next few days.

## 3.2 Brake system warning light (red) [safety] [stop]
Meaning: The brake fluid level is low or there is a fault in the brake system. This light also comes on while the parking brake is applied.
Required action: If the light stays on after releasing the parking brake, stop safely when it is safe to do so. Do not continue driving. Contact Veloxa service or roadside assistance.

## 3.3 ABS warning light (amber) [safety]
Meaning: The anti-lock braking system has a fault. Normal braking remains available but the wheels may lock under hard braking.
Required action: Drive carefully, allow a longer stopping distance, and contact Veloxa service as soon as possible.

## 3.4 Airbag (SRS) warning light (red) [safety]
Meaning: The airbag or seat belt pretensioner system has a fault. The airbags may not deploy in a collision.
Required action: Contact Veloxa service as soon as possible to have the system inspected by a certified technician. Do not attempt to repair or disconnect any airbag component.

## 3.5 Glow plug indicator (amber)
Meaning: Steady when the ignition is switched on in cold weather: the glow plugs are pre-heating. Flashing while driving: an engine management fault.
Required action: Wait for the steady light to go out before starting the engine. If it flashes while driving, contact Veloxa service.

## 3.6 Diesel particulate filter (DPF) light (amber)
Meaning: Soot is building up in the diesel particulate filter because the vehicle has been used mostly for short journeys.
Required action: When it is safe and legal to do so, drive at a steady speed above 60 km/h for about 15 minutes to allow the filter to clean itself. If the light stays on, contact Veloxa service.

## 3.7 AdBlue / SCR warning light (amber)
Meaning: The AdBlue level is low or the emissions reduction system has a fault. If ignored, the engine may not restart once switched off.
Required action: Refill AdBlue at the next opportunity. If the light stays on after refilling, contact Veloxa service. Do not attempt to disable the emissions system.

## 3.8 Water in fuel warning light (amber)
Meaning: Water has collected in the fuel filter.
Required action: Contact Veloxa service soon to have the fuel filter drained. Continued driving may damage the injection system.

## 3.9 Oil pressure warning light (red) [stop]
Meaning: Engine oil pressure is too low.
Required action: Stop safely as soon as it is safe to do so and switch off the engine. Do not continue driving. Check the oil level when the engine is cold and contact Veloxa service.

## 3.10 Fuel smell or fuel leak [safety] [stop]
If you smell fuel inside or around the vehicle, or see fuel leaking:
Required action: Stop safely as soon as it is safe to do so and switch off the engine. Do not smoke or use naked flames. Move away from the vehicle and contact Veloxa service or roadside assistance. Do not continue driving.

## 5.1 Maintenance schedule
Scheduled service is due every 20,000 km or 12 months, whichever comes first.

| Item | Interval |
|---|---|
| Engine oil and oil filter | Every service |
| Fuel filter (drain water) | Every service |
| Fuel filter (replace) | Every 40,000 km |
| Air filter | Every 40,000 km or 24 months |
| Brake fluid | Every 24 months |

## 5.2 Engine oil specification
Use Veloxa Spec VS-OIL-05 (low-ash, DPF compatible), grade 5W-30. Oil capacity with filter change: 5.6 litres.

## 6.1 Tyre pressures (cold)
| Load | Front | Rear |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 3 people | 240 kPa (35 psi) | 240 kPa (35 psi) |
| Full load or towing | 250 kPa (36 psi) | 280 kPa (41 psi) |

## 6.2 Tyre damage or puncture [safety] [stop]
If you feel a sudden vibration, hear a loud noise from a tyre, or the vehicle pulls to one side, a tyre may be damaged.
Required action: Hold the steering wheel firmly, reduce speed gradually, and stop safely when it is safe to do so. Do not continue driving on a damaged tyre. Fit the spare wheel or contact roadside assistance.
