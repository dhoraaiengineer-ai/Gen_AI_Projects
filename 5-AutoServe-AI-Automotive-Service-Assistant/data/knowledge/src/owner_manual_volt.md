## 1.1 About this manual
This is a SYNTHETIC excerpt for the fictional Veloxa Volt battery-electric crossover. It is for demonstration only and must not be used for any real vehicle.

## 1.2 High-voltage safety [safety]
The Volt contains a high-voltage (HV) battery and HV cables with orange covers. HV components must only be serviced by a certified HV technician.
Required action: Never open, probe, cut, or repair the HV battery, HV cables, or orange-covered components yourself. Contact Veloxa service.

## 3.1 HV system fault warning light (red) [safety] [stop]
Meaning: A fault has been detected in the high-voltage system.
Required action: Stop safely as soon as it is safe to do so and switch the vehicle off. Do not continue driving. Contact Veloxa service or roadside assistance. Do not touch any HV component.

## 3.2 HV battery temperature warning light (red) [safety] [stop]
Meaning: The HV battery temperature is outside its safe range.
Required action: Stop safely as soon as it is safe to do so and switch the vehicle off. If you notice smoke, a burning smell, hissing or popping sounds, or heat from under the vehicle: stop the vehicle away from buildings, get everyone out, move away from the vehicle, and call emergency services. Then contact Veloxa roadside assistance.

## 3.3 Reduced power indicator (amber) [safety]
Meaning: Drive power has been limited to protect the HV battery or drive motor, for example when the battery charge is very low or the battery is very cold or hot.
Required action: Drive gently to a safe place or charging point. If the indicator stays on after charging, contact Veloxa service.

## 3.4 12 V battery warning light (red)
Meaning: The 12 V auxiliary battery is not being charged.
Required action: Switch off non-essential electrical equipment and drive to the nearest Veloxa service centre.

## 3.5 Brake system warning light (red) [safety] [stop]
Meaning: The brake fluid level is low or there is a fault in the brake system, including the regenerative braking system.
Required action: If the light stays on after releasing the parking brake, stop safely when it is safe to do so. Do not continue driving. Contact Veloxa service or roadside assistance.

## 3.6 Airbag (SRS) warning light (red) [safety]
Meaning: The airbag or seat belt pretensioner system has a fault. The airbags may not deploy in a collision.
Required action: Contact Veloxa service as soon as possible to have the system inspected by a certified technician. Do not attempt to repair or disconnect any airbag component.

## 3.7 Tyre pressure warning light (amber) [safety]
Meaning: One or more tyres are significantly under-inflated.
Required action: Reduce speed and check the tyre pressures at the next safe opportunity. Inflate to the pressures in section 6.1.

## 3.8 Driver assistance (ADAS) warning light (amber) [safety]
Meaning: The front camera or radar is blocked, misaligned, or faulty. Lane keeping and automatic emergency braking may not work.
Required action: Clean the windscreen camera area and the front radar cover. If the light stays on, contact Veloxa service. After a windscreen replacement or front-end repair, the camera and radar must be recalibrated by Veloxa service.

## 4.1 Charging at home (AC)
Use the supplied Mode 2 cable or a Veloxa-approved wall box. AC charging from 10% to 80% takes about 6 hours on a 7.4 kW wall box.
For daily use, Veloxa recommends setting the charge limit to 80% to reduce battery ageing. Charging stops automatically when the charge limit is reached. Set the limit to 100% before a long journey.

## 4.2 DC fast charging
DC fast charging from 10% to 80% takes about 30 minutes at a 100 kW charger. Charging slows above 80% to protect the battery. Avoid using DC fast charging for every charge.

## 4.3 Charge port light colours
| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| White | Port lit, ready to connect |
| Blinking green | Charging |
| Solid green | Charging complete |
| Red | Charging fault — unplug, check the cable, and try again |

## 5.1 Maintenance schedule
Scheduled service is due every 30,000 km or 24 months, whichever comes first.

| Item | Interval |
|---|---|
| Brake fluid | Every 24 months |
| Cabin air filter | Every service |
| HV battery coolant | Every 160,000 km or 8 years |
| Tyre rotation | Every 10,000 km |
