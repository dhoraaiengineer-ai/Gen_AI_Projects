## P0300 Random or multiple cylinder misfire detected
Applies to: Aria. See service manual section 7.2.

## P0301 Cylinder 1 misfire detected
Applies to: Aria. See service manual section 7.2.

## P0302 Cylinder 2 misfire detected
Applies to: Aria. See service manual section 7.2.

## P0303 Cylinder 3 misfire detected
Applies to: Aria. See service manual section 7.2.

## P0304 Cylinder 4 misfire detected
Applies to: Aria. See service manual section 7.2.

## P0171 System too lean (bank 1)
Applies to: Aria. Check for intake air leaks and fuel pressure.

## P0420 Catalyst system efficiency below threshold (bank 1)
Applies to: Aria. Check for misfire codes first; repair misfires before replacing the catalyst.

## P0455 Evaporative emission system large leak detected
Applies to: Aria. See service manual section 7.4.

## P0380 Glow plug circuit malfunction
Applies to: Terra. See service manual section 7.3.

## P0401 Exhaust gas recirculation flow insufficient
Applies to: Terra. See service manual section 7.4.

## P2463 Diesel particulate filter soot accumulation
Applies to: Terra. See service manual section 7.1.

## P20EE SCR NOx catalyst efficiency below threshold
Applies to: Terra. Check AdBlue quality and the NOx sensors. Do not disable the SCR system.

## P0D27 Onboard charger input voltage out of range
Applies to: Volt. See service manual section 7.3.

## P0AFA HV battery system voltage low
Applies to: Volt. Certified HV technician required. See service manual section 7.4.

## C0035 Left front wheel speed sensor circuit
Applies to: all models. Inspect the sensor, wiring, and tone ring. Affects ABS and stability control.

## C0040 Right front wheel speed sensor circuit
Applies to: all models. Inspect the sensor, wiring, and tone ring. Affects ABS and stability control.

## B0001 Driver frontal airbag stage 1 deployment control
Applies to: all models. Certified technician required. Follow SRS safety procedures. Do not probe airbag connectors.

## B1A23 A/C condenser fan relay circuit
Applies to: Aria. See TSB-AR-2025-02.

## U0100 Lost communication with engine control module
Applies to: all models. Check CAN bus wiring and ECM power supply.

## U0164 Lost communication with HVAC control module
Applies to: Volt. See TSB-VT-2025-02 for infotainment and HVAC software.
