## RC-2025-01 Fuel return line retaining clip [safety] [stop]
Affected vehicles: Veloxa Terra, model years 2022–2023, VIN serials 100001–100010 (SYNTHETIC).
Issue: The retaining clip on the fuel return line may not be fully seated. Over time the line can loosen and leak fuel, which increases the risk of fire.
What owners should do: Contact Veloxa service to book the free recall repair. If you smell fuel, stop safely as soon as it is safe to do so, switch off the engine, do not smoke, move away from the vehicle, and contact Veloxa service or roadside assistance.
Remedy: Veloxa service will inspect and replace the clip free of charge (op code OP-RC2501-INSPECT, part VLX-FL-CLIP-02).
