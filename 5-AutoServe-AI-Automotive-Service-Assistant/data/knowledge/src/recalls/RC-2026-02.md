## RC-2026-02 HV battery coolant hose clamp [safety]
Affected vehicles: Veloxa Volt, model years 2024–2025, VIN serials 100011–100020 (SYNTHETIC).
Issue: A coolant hose clamp at the HV battery inlet may lose tension. A coolant leak could reduce battery cooling and, in rare cases, trigger the HV battery temperature warning.
What owners should do: Contact Veloxa service to book the free recall repair. If the HV battery temperature warning light comes on, follow the owner's manual section 3.2. Do not inspect or touch HV components yourself.
Remedy: A certified HV technician will replace the clamp free of charge (op code OP-RC2602-CLAMP, part VLX-HV-CLAMP-11).
