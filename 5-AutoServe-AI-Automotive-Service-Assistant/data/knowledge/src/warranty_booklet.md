## 1.1 About this booklet
This is a SYNTHETIC warranty booklet for the fictional OEM Veloxa Motors. It is for demonstration only. Coverage starts on the in-service date recorded when the vehicle is first registered.

## 2.1 Basic warranty
Veloxa Motors covers defects in materials and workmanship for 3 years or 100,000 km, whichever comes first.

## 2.2 Powertrain warranty
The engine, transmission, drive axles and, for diesel vehicles, the turbocharger are covered for 5 years or 150,000 km, whichever comes first.

## 2.3 EV high-voltage battery warranty
For Veloxa Volt vehicles, the HV battery is covered against defects for 8 years or 160,000 km, whichever comes first. During this period, the HV battery is also covered if its usable capacity (state of health) falls below 70%.

## 3.1 Exclusions
The warranty does not cover:
- Wear items: brake pads, brake discs, wiper blades, tyres, clutch disc, bulbs, and filters.
- Damage caused by accidents, misuse, flood, or fire.
- Failures caused by aftermarket modifications or non-Veloxa parts.
- Failures caused by missed scheduled maintenance (section 3.2).

## 3.2 Maintenance requirements
Scheduled maintenance must be carried out at the intervals in the owner's manual. A service is on time if it is done within 1,000 km or 30 days of the due point, whichever is reached first. If a service is missed beyond this tolerance, failures of components related to that service are excluded.

## 4.1 Claim documents required
A warranty claim must include:
- The repair order with the customer's complaint, the cause, and the correction.
- The parts invoice.
- A diagnostic report with the DTC printout (for electrical or engine faults).
- An odometer photo taken at check-in.
For EV HV battery capacity claims, also include the battery state-of-health report.
For recall work, reference the recall campaign code instead of submitting a warranty claim.

## 4.2 Labour time on claims
Labour is paid at the flat-rate time in the Veloxa labour time guide. Labour claimed above the flat-rate time by more than 10% requires a written justification.
