## 1.1 About this manual
This is a SYNTHETIC excerpt for the fictional Veloxa Aria petrol hatchback. It is for demonstration only and must not be used for any real vehicle.

## 3.1 Engine warning light (amber)
Meaning: The engine management system has detected a fault that may affect emissions or performance.
Required action: Reduce speed and avoid heavy acceleration. Contact Veloxa service within the next few days. If the light flashes, stop safely when it is safe to do so and contact Veloxa service — a flashing light can mean a misfire that may damage the catalytic converter.

## 3.2 Brake system warning light (red) [safety] [stop]
Meaning: The brake fluid level is low or there is a fault in the brake system. This light also comes on while the parking brake is applied.
Required action: If the light stays on after releasing the parking brake, stop safely when it is safe to do so. Do not continue driving. Contact Veloxa service or roadside assistance.

## 3.3 ABS warning light (amber) [safety]
Meaning: The anti-lock braking system has a fault. Normal braking remains available but the wheels may lock under hard braking.
Required action: Drive carefully, allow a longer stopping distance, and contact Veloxa service as soon as possible.

## 3.4 Airbag (SRS) warning light (red) [safety]
Meaning: The airbag or seat belt pretensioner system has a fault. The airbags may not deploy in a collision.
Required action: Contact Veloxa service as soon as possible to have the system inspected by a certified technician. Do not attempt to repair or disconnect any airbag component.

## 3.5 Tyre pressure warning light (amber) [safety]
Meaning: One or more tyres are significantly under-inflated.
Required action: Reduce speed and check the tyre pressures at the next safe opportunity. Inflate to the pressures in section 6.1. If a tyre is damaged or loses pressure quickly, follow section 6.2.

## 3.6 Oil pressure warning light (red) [stop]
Meaning: Engine oil pressure is too low.
Required action: Stop safely as soon as it is safe to do so and switch off the engine. Do not continue driving. Check the oil level when the engine is cold and contact Veloxa service.

## 3.7 Coolant temperature warning light (red) [stop]
Meaning: The engine is overheating.
Required action: Stop safely as soon as it is safe to do so and switch off the engine. Do not open the coolant reservoir cap while the engine is hot. Contact Veloxa service.

## 3.8 Charging system warning light (red)
Meaning: The 12 V battery is not being charged.
Required action: Switch off non-essential electrical equipment and drive to the nearest Veloxa service centre. The vehicle may stop running when the battery is discharged.

## 3.9 Seat belt reminder light (red) [safety]
Meaning: A front seat belt is not fastened while the vehicle is moving.
Required action: Fasten all seat belts. If the light stays on with all belts fastened, contact Veloxa service.

## 3.10 Electric power steering warning light (amber) [safety]
Meaning: The power steering system has a fault. Steering may feel heavier.
Required action: Drive carefully and contact Veloxa service as soon as possible. If steering becomes very heavy, stop safely when it is safe to do so.

## 5.1 Maintenance schedule
Scheduled service is due every 15,000 km or 12 months, whichever comes first.

| Item | Interval |
|---|---|
| Engine oil and oil filter | Every service |
| Air filter | Every 30,000 km or 24 months |
| Spark plugs | Every 60,000 km |
| Brake fluid | Every 24 months |
| Cabin air filter | Every service |

## 5.2 Engine oil specification
Use Veloxa Spec VS-OIL-02, grade 0W-20. Oil capacity with filter change: 3.8 litres.

## 6.1 Tyre pressures (cold)
| Position | Pressure |
|---|---|
| Front | 230 kPa (33 psi) |
| Rear | 220 kPa (32 psi) |
| Temporary spare | 420 kPa (61 psi) |

## 6.2 Tyre damage or puncture [safety] [stop]
If you feel a sudden vibration, hear a loud noise from a tyre, or the vehicle pulls to one side, a tyre may be damaged.
Required action: Hold the steering wheel firmly, reduce speed gradually, and stop safely when it is safe to do so. Do not continue driving on a damaged tyre. Fit the temporary spare or contact roadside assistance.
