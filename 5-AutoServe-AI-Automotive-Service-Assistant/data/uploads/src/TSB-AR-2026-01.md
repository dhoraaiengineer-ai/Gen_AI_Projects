## TSB-AR-2026-01 Rear wiper stops mid-stroke
Affected vehicles: Veloxa Aria, model years 2023–2025, VIN serials 100001–100060 (SYNTHETIC).
Symptom: The rear wiper stops partway across the glass and does not return to the parked position, usually in freezing weather. B1C45 may be stored.
Cause: Water can enter the rear wiper motor connector and freeze, causing an intermittent ground connection.
Correction: Dry the connector, fit the sealed connector boot VLX-WPR-BOOT-01, and apply dielectric grease. Op code OP-WIPER. Replace the rear wiper motor only if the fault continues after this repair.
